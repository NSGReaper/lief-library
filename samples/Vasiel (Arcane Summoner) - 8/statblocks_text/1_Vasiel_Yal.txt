Vasiel has been blessed by Milani because of actions he took to aid a group of half elves. They had banded together for protection and were being persecuted by local townsfolk. He left to live in Varisia because of the aftermath of this event.

Spent decades studying varisian history and ancient Thassilon. Now he is the Guest Lecturer of Thassilonian Grammar at the Acadamae in Korvosa.
Has grown disenfranchised with the pure theory work when he began to doubt the validity of many of the sources as they were third hand or worse. 
Took a refresher Conjuration course but designed a self corriculum based on the Thassilonian School of Sloth allowed him to combine his interests.
Joined an expedition to investigate some recently unearthed Thasillonian ruins. 
Took ill on the journey there and was left in Sandpoint to recover.
Has now recovered but has no way to catch up with the caravan.
